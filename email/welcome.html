Consistency shows which of the five you keep best — and which you don’t.

The five are not a goal you set for yourself — they are decreed, five appointments a day already written for you. Gul exists for one reason: to help you keep every one of them, every day.

Guard each prayer as it comes, and be constant — the most beloved deeds are the ones kept steadily. Let the fully opened flower be your habit, not your exception.
