Gul

Pray your five

Gul is a prayer tracker. Five prayers, five petals — one opens each time you pray, and a day fully prayed is a flower in bloom.

Gul means flower. The app shows the five daily prayer times for wherever you are, and gives you a quiet, private place to note the ones you have prayed. That is the whole of it. There is no feed, no streak to defend in public, and nobody else can see your record.

What it does

  • Prayer times where you are. Calculated on your own device from the position of the sun, using the calculation method you choose.
  • A place to note your five. One tap marks a prayer and opens a petal.
  • A record you can look back on. Each day becomes its own small flower, and you can correct any past day.
  • It works offline and without an account. Signing in is optional; it exists so your record survives a lost phone and appears on your other devices.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. Gul works fully without an account, keeping everything on the device you are using.

If you choose to sign in with Google, Gul receives your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account. It uses them for one purpose: to identify your account so your prayer record can be saved and synced back to you. Gul does not post anything to your Google account, does not read your contacts, calendar, Drive or mail, and does not request any Google permission beyond basic sign-in.

Your prayer record is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone.
