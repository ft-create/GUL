01 / The mark

Five domes radiating from the sun. The petal is not like a dome — it is the dome.

The dome silhouette, stretched: flat base, haunches swelling past the base, a point at the top. Each dome belongs to one named prayer and is fixed to its position — Fajr at the top, then clockwise — so the shape of a day is legible at a glance. A prayer you have noted is a dome filled cream; one you have not is the same dome, outlined. Never smaller, never wilted, never red. Five-fold symmetry holds at every state, so the mark can never score anyone.

Five noted · two noted · Isha through to first light — a grey disc, never a crescent

02 / Design + identity

The sun is the palette. Everything else is a small fixed set.

Gul (گُل) is the rose. The Qur'an's reward for those who guard their prayers is a garden — Gul is what grows in it. Deliberately not a crescent: the crescent-and-star is the most-used mark in this category and is Ottoman heraldry rather than anything scriptural, so reaching for it buys recognition and spends distinctiveness in the same move.

−40° to +70° · the twilight stops sit near −22°, where Fajr and Isha fall

Eleven stops interpolated by real solar altitude — computed, not chosen — and the setting sun runs warmer than the rising sun at the same angle, because dusk should not look like dawn. From Isha to first light the disc turns grey (#9AA6AE): the same circle, only its colour changes, so the mark never changes shape. Nothing else in the product may be warm.

Gold is the sun and nothing else. No gold buttons, no gold borders. Green means a prayer was noted and nothing else. A lapse is earth. There is no red anywhere in the product.

02

Type does one job each. Instrument Serif for prayer names, times and anything meant to be read slowly. DM Sans for every interface element, labels uppercase at .22em. Amiri for Qur'anic Arabic at double line height — never animated, never decorative, never tappable.

03

The voice is plain, quiet, second person. Never promise reward, standing or acceptance. No streak language. Never "don't miss a prayer" — a threat dressed as a benefit.

03 / The motion law

Two families of motion, never mixed.

If a pixel moves, it moves because the sun moved — or because a person did. The sun is linear: it does not accelerate for anybody, and it moves whether or not the app is open. A petal is eased: it answers a person, so it moves like one — and only when the person notes a prayer. prefers-reduced-motion collapses every transition to 1ms; the state still changes, it simply arrives instead of travelling.

600msThe sun — LINEAR. Radius, colour, glow. Never eases, never waits.
760msA petal — EASED on cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1). Fires only when a prayer is noted, never on a timer.
200msThe splash stagger — petals open one at a time on launch, landing on the day's true state. 3.0s total.

What the animation must never do: auto-mark a prayer. Wilt, shrink or redden an unnoted petal. Celebrate the fifth. Count anything. An unnoted prayer is an outlined dome and nothing more.

04 / The brief

Make prayer easy to keep without making it something to score.

Purpose built

Prayer times that are locally accurate, reminders on the user's own terms, and a quiet private place to note five prayers a day — with no claim about anyone's standing.

The product answer

A day is one flower. Note a prayer and its dome fills; the sun at the centre reports what the sky is actually doing. The record is the reward.

What it refuses to do

No streak, no percentage, no perfect day, no aggregate anywhere. Nothing is auto-marked. A window that passed unnoted is simply unopened, and that is all.

Privacy posture

Records live on the device and work with no account and no network. Accounts are opt-in: sign in and your notes sync, encrypted in transit, readable only by you. Coordinates are used locally and never uploaded.

Accuracy posture

Times are computed astronomically on the device — NOAA/Meeus solar position, ten common conventions, Standard and Ḥanafī Asr, and honest high-latitude rules that always say when they have estimated. No API key, works offline, anywhere on Earth.

The line it holds

The app cannot know whether a prayer happened, so it never says one did. No claim of divine reward, spiritual standing or prayer validity — questions of practice go to a qualified scholar, not to software.

05 / Product path

From a working web app to a build that can wake you at Fajr.

Now — the web app

Prayer times anywhere on Earth, accounts and cross-device sync, marking, history and the live mark all work in the browser. It installs to a home screen and runs offline.

Next — the native build

Scheduled reminders are the reason the native build exists: a web page cannot reliably wake anyone before dawn. Same mark, same engine, same account.

Market-ready release

Account recovery, consent and privacy documentation, a licensed Adhan recitation with its attribution, and store distribution.

Status. The web app is live and in use, with accounts and sync. The native build and the store release are a deliberate next phase, not a claim already made.
