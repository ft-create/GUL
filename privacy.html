Privacy Policy

Gul · Effective 2 August 2026

Gul records something private: whether you prayed. This page says exactly what happens to that, to your location, and to your Google account details — in the same plain words the app uses.

Gul is made by Fareed Tareen. Contact for anything on this page: ft@fareedtareen.com.

The short version

  • Gul works fully without an account. Used that way, nothing you enter leaves your device.
  • Your prayer record is private to your account. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with other users or third parties.
  • You never have to give Gul your location. You can pick a city from a built-in list instead.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app, at any time.

Location

You are never required to share it

Prayer times depend on where you are. Gul offers two ways to establish that, and the choice is always yours:

  • Choose a city. Gul carries a built-in list of cities and their coordinates. Choosing one involves no location permission and no request to your device's location services at all.
  • Use your precise location. Only if you press the button that says so. Gul then asks your browser for your position, and your browser asks you. If you decline, the app carries on with the city you chose.

Gul does not request your location on load, does not track you in the background, and has no ability to read your position unless you press that button and your browser grants it.

What is kept, and where

Whichever way you choose, Gul keeps a single place: a name, a latitude and longitude, and a time zone. When you use precise location, the coordinates are rounded to four decimal places before being stored — roughly a city block, which is as much precision as a prayer time needs.

This place is saved on your device. If you are signed in, it is also saved to your account in the cloud, as part of your settings, so that your other devices show the same times. To be exact about this: when you are signed in, your chosen place — including precise coordinates, if you used them — does leave your device and is stored in Gul's database under your account.

It is stored so your own devices can read it back. It is not shared with anyone, not sold, and not used to build any profile of you.

What is not sent anywhere

Prayer times themselves are computed on your device, from the stored place, by arithmetic in the app. No location lookup service is called, and no request containing your position is made to any third party for the purpose of calculating times.

Your prayer record

The five daily marks are the heart of the app, and they describe your religious practice. Gul treats them accordingly.

For each day you note, Gul stores which of the five prayers you marked and when the record was last changed. Nothing else — no place, no time of day at which you tapped, no device details attached to the mark.

Signed out, this lives only in your browser's storage on that device. Signed in, it is stored in your account so it survives a lost phone and appears on your other devices.

Your record is private to your account. It is never sold, never used for advertising, never used to train anything, and never shared with other users or with third parties. It is not aggregated into public statistics.

Your Google account, if you use it

Signing in with Google is optional. If you use it, Gul receives your name, email address and profile picture from Google, and uses them only to identify your account and to show you which account you are signed into. Gul requests no other Google permission — it cannot read your mail, contacts, calendar or files, and cannot post anything on your behalf.

You may also create an account with an email address and password instead. In that case Gul stores your email address; the password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is never visible to Gul or to its maker.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Gul's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained from Google is used only to provide and improve the user-facing features described on this page, is not transferred to others except as required to operate the service or by law, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans except with your explicit consent, for security purposes, or where required by law.

Who processes data on Gul's behalf

These providers process data to run the service and nothing else. Gul has no analytics, no advertising network, no tracking pixels and no third-party scripts beyond the font files above.

Email

Creating an account sends you one welcome email. Creating an account with an email address also sends a verification message so a mistyped address is caught early. Gul does not send marketing email and has no mailing list.

How long it is kept

Your record is kept for as long as your account exists. Delete the account and it goes with it. Data stored only on your device stays there until you clear your browser storage or remove the app.

Getting your data out, and deleting it

Deleting. Open Settings in the app and choose Delete my account. This erases your account and every prayer record in it, on every device. It cannot be undone. Notes held only on the device you are using remain there until you clear them, and the app tells you so before you confirm.

A copy of your data. Email ft@fareedtareen.com from the address on your account and you will be sent your record.

Children

Gul is not directed at children under 13, and Gul does not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, write to the address above and it will be removed.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Access to your stored record is restricted to your own signed-in account by the database's security rules. No system is perfect, and Gul will not claim otherwise.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top changes with it, and material changes will be announced in the app.
